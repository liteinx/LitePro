## 📖 How to Use

### Starting a Conversation

1. **Log in** to your account
2. **Type a name** in the search box at the top
3. **Click "Start Chat"** next to the person you want to talk to
4. **Start typing!**

That's all there is to it. No complicated friend requests, no waiting for approvals.

### Sharing Files

Click the **📎 File** button under the chat box, select your file, and it's sent! You can even send multiple files at once.

### Viewing Past Conversations

Use the date dropdown at the top of your chat to see conversations from previous days. Your entire chat history is just a click away.
